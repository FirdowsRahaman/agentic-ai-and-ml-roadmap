## 1. Essential Textbooks (Read Selectively alongside Building)
- **[Designing Machine Learning Systems](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/designing-machine-learning/9781098107956/)** & **[AI Engineering](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/ai-engineering/9781098166298/)** by Chip Huyen: The definitive industry guides to production MLOps, feature stores, drift, RAG, evaluation, and LLM system design.
- **[Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras, and PyTorch](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/hands-on-machine-learning/9781098125967/)** by Aurélien Géron: Practical implementation reference for classical ML, ensembles, and neural networks.
- **[Understanding Deep Learning](https://udlbook.github.io/udlbook/)** by Simon J.D. Prince *(Free PDF)*: Crystal-clear modern mathematical treatment of Transformers, Diffusion, Residual Networks, and RL.

## 2. Top Video Courses & Visual Intuition
- **[Andrej Karpathy: Neural Networks: Zero to Hero](https://karpathy.ai/zero-to-hero.html)**: Build `micrograd`, `makemore`, `GPT-2`, and a BPE tokenizer (`minbpe`) from scratch in Python/PyTorch.
- **[3Blue1Brown: Essence of Linear Algebra & Deep Learning](https://www.3blue1brown.com/)**: Best visual intuition for matrices, backpropagation, and Transformer attention.
- **[StatQuest with Josh Starmer](https://statquest.org/)**: Step-by-step visual breakdowns of `XGBoost`, `PCA`, `ROC/PR` curves, and statistics.
- **[Made With ML](https://madewithml.com/)** by Goku Mohandas: Production ML engineering lifecycle (packaging, testing, tracking, serving, CI/CD).

## 3. 8 Landmark Papers & Engineering Guides Every Candidate Should Know
1. **Transformers:** *[Attention Is All You Need](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762)* (Vaswani et al., 2017)
2. **Residual Skip Connections:** *[Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.03385)* (He et al., 2015)
3. **Multimodal Alignment (`CLIP`):** *[Learning Transferable Visual Models From Natural Language Supervision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.00020)* (Radford et al., 2021)
4. **Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (`LoRA`):** *[LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685)* (Hu et al., 2021)
5. **RAG:** *[Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.11401)* (Lewis et al., 2020)
6. **Agentic Reasoning (`ReAct`):** *[ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629)* (Yao et al., 2022)
7. **LLM Serving (`PagedAttention` / `vLLM`):** *[Efficient Memory Management for Large Language Model Serving with PagedAttention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.06180)* (Kwon et al., 2023)
8. **Agentic Design Patterns:** *[Building Effective Agents](https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents)* (Anthropic Engineering Guide) & *[Model Context Protocol Specification](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/)*
