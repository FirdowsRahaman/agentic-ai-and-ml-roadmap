## Interactive & Visual Courses
- **[Neural Networks: Zero to Hero](https://karpathy.ai/zero-to-hero.html)** by Andrej Karpathy  
  *Focus:* Building `micrograd`, `makemore`, GPT tokenizers, and full GPT-2 architectures from scratch in Python/PyTorch.
- **[Essence of Linear Algebra & Neural Networks](https://www.3blue1brown.com/)** by Grant Sanderson (3Blue1Brown)  
  *Focus:* Geometric intuition for eigenvectors, linear transformations, gradient descent, and attention heads.
- **[StatQuest with Josh Starmer](https://statquest.org/)**  
  *Focus:* Intuitive visual explanations of XGBoost, PCA, ROC/PR curves, and hypothesis testing.
- **[Made With ML](https://madewithml.com/)** by Goku Mohandas  
  *Focus:* The full production lifecycle: packaging, testing, experiment tracking with MLflow, serving with FastAPI, and CI/CD automation.

## Academic & Specialization Tracks
- **[CS229: Machine Learning](https://cs229.stanford.edu/)** (Stanford University)  
  *Focus:* Supervised and unsupervised learning, VC dimension, generalization theory, and kernel methods.
- **[CS224N: Natural Language Processing with Deep Learning](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224n/)** (Stanford University)  
  *Focus:* Word embeddings, self-attention, transformers, pretrained foundation models, and prompt tuning.
- **[CS25: Transformers United](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs25/)** (Stanford University)  
  *Focus:* Seminar series on bleeding-edge transformer applications in language, vision, audio, robotics, and agents.
