## Production ML & AI Systems
- **[Designing Machine Learning Systems](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/designing-machine-learning/9781098107956/)** by Chip Huyen  
  *Focus:* Production architectures, feature engineering, offline/online data consistency, model monitoring, and continuous training loops.
- **[AI Engineering: Building Applications with Foundation Models](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/ai-engineering/9781098166298/)** by Chip Huyen  
  *Focus:* LLM evaluation harnesses, structured prompting, context optimization, production RAG, and latency SLAs.

## Machine Learning & Deep Learning Foundations
- **[Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras, and PyTorch (3rd Edition)](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/hands-on-machine-learning/9781098125967/)** by Aurélien Géron  
  *Focus:* End-to-end practical workflows, hyperparameter tuning, ensemble methods, and neural network fundamentals.
- **[Understanding Deep Learning](https://udlbook.github.io/udlbook/)** by Simon J.D. Prince *(Free PDF)*  
  *Focus:* Rigorous mathematical and visual foundations of residual networks, attention mechanisms, diffusion models, and reinforcement learning.

## Mathematics & Algorithmic Foundations
- **[Mathematics for Machine Learning](https://mml-book.github.io/)** by Marc Peter Deisenroth, A. Aldo Faisal, and Cheng Soon Ong *(Free PDF)*  
  *Focus:* Linear algebra, analytic geometry, matrix decompositions, vector calculus, and probability distributions.
- **[Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/pattern-recognition-and-machine-learning/)** by Christopher M. Bishop  
  *Focus:* Bayesian inference, graphical models, and kernel methods.
